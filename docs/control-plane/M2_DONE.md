5. **Named limits accepted (exact wordings in `M2_DEMO.md` section 7 and ADR 0001):**
   - 7.1 (F-8): "M2: the control plane signs and stores a bundle on one admin's request, retaining and serving its change approvals verbatim, without verifying them. No connector applies such a bundle: the connector verifies two distinct CLI approvals against the pinned registry before any loosening takes effect, and the control plane refuses operator self-approval regardless of the bundle's `allow_requester` flag."
   - 7.6: "M2: the connector counts any two distinct CLI approvers against its pinned registry; it cannot see who proposed the bundle, because the signed envelope carries no proposer. Proposer exclusion (ADR 0001 A2.3) is enforced only by the control plane at `POST /api/v1/admin/bundles`, which refuses an approval whose key belongs to the publishing admin (ADR 0001 Amendment A2). Two different humans still approve every loosening on the laptop; the three-person separation of A2.3 depends on the control plane."
   - 7.7 (G7-C): "Quarantine is a server-side state for forked records (`audit_quarantine`). In M2 the connector does not enter quarantine; it raises a local `audit_fork` alert and keeps enforcing locally. Device-level quarantine is M3."
   - Unsigned `high_water.json` (25 Oct, Low), with the 4 Nov sentence: "with the bootstrap loader, a host-level writer who deletes `bundle.json` and `policy_history/` and zeroes `high_water.json` makes the connector re-apply the server's sequence 1 bundle (a rollback to the first approved bundle) instead of locking; this is the same capability the limit already grants (restoring an old `bundle.json` from `policy_history/`), and every such bundle still carries two verified CLI approvals."
   - 25 Oct rulings still standing: every remote approval requires dual until command classification lands (R8 strict xfail); the broker passes only an env allowlist; the F3 operator limit is Low and fixed in M3 (ADR 0010 item 6, amended 4 Nov).
