## Gate observation (functional, fails closed; not a vulnerability)

The M2 connector sync path has no product entry point. There is no `sandbox enroll`/`sync`/`signer` CLI command (`src/sandbox/cli/`). Nothing calls `run_once` or `apply_external`. `_fetch_decisions` (`process.py:350-372`) drops the decisions. `_upload_escalations` reads `.sandbox/pending/`, which the hook never writes (it writes `escalations/pending`). In `ee/web`, `App.tsx` has no inbox, and `approval.ts:89` signs with a fresh key each time, not the IndexedDB key. Today a managed laptop simply never gets a remote approval (fail closed). But demo steps 3, 5 and 11 cannot pass without this wiring. The wiring must land with H1-H3 and M2 fixed, and it needs its own security review.

### 3. Named limits accepted by the founder (4 Nov 2026, session 9)

The exact wordings live in `docs/control-plane/M2_DEMO.md` section 7 and are copied here so this file stands alone.

7.1 (F-8, Medium; the full fix is M3): "M2: the control plane signs and stores a bundle on one admin's request, retaining and serving its change approvals verbatim, without verifying them. No connector applies such a bundle: the connector verifies two distinct CLI approvals against the pinned registry before any loosening takes effect, and the control plane refuses operator self-approval regardless of the bundle's `allow_requester` flag." Also in ADR 0001 Amendment A1 "Named limit".

7.6 (proposer exclusion, Low): "M2: the connector counts any two distinct CLI approvers against its pinned registry; it cannot see who proposed the bundle, because the signed envelope carries no proposer. Proposer exclusion (ADR 0001 A2.3) is enforced only by the control plane at `POST /api/v1/admin/bundles`, which refuses an approval whose key belongs to the publishing admin (ADR 0001 Amendment A2). Two different humans still approve every loosening on the laptop; the three-person separation of A2.3 depends on the control plane."

7.7 (G7-C, Low): "Quarantine is a server-side state for forked records (`audit_quarantine`). In M2 the connector does not enter quarantine; it raises a local `audit_fork` alert and keeps enforcing locally. Device-level quarantine is M3." Also ADR 0001 Amendment A3 item 5.

Unsigned `high_water.json` (founder, 25 Oct, Low; HANDOFF "Founder rulings (25 Oct 2026, terminal)" item 3 and "M2 T12b-1" item 4b), with the sentence added on 4 Nov at the end of M2_DEMO 7.7: "with the bootstrap loader, a host-level writer who deletes `bundle.json` and `policy_history/` and zeroes `high_water.json` makes the connector re-apply the server's sequence 1 bundle (a rollback to the first approved bundle) instead of locking; this is the same capability the limit already grants (restoring an old `bundle.json` from `policy_history/`), and every such bundle still carries two verified CLI approvals."
